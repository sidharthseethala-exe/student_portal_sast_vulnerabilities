INTERPRETATION
---------------
A p-value is not the probability that a generator is secure. It measures compatibility with a particular statistical null model. Small p-values can indicate a discrepancy, while large p-values do not prove randomness. Multiple tests, sample size, dependencies, and the cryptographic design must also be considered.
